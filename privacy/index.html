PROGRAM INFORMATION · NOVEMBER 2026

Privacy & participation

NITE Academy is offered by NITE Run Delivery LLC. This page describes the current pilot experience and should be reviewed and finalized with legal counsel before full commercial launch.

What we collect

When you register, the authentication provider processes your email address and password. The Academy stores your preferred name, selling experience, self-reported Amazon plan and Seller ID, product interests, consignment review requests, and any future confirmed allocations or settlement records. Do not submit passwords, bank credentials, Social Security numbers, government identification images, or customer financial data.

How data is used

We use it to authenticate members, provide a private dashboard, review eligibility for consignment opportunities, communicate about applications, and account for transactions if commercial partnerships are later approved. Authentication and data hosting use Supabase. Your Seller ID entry does not authenticate, validate, or link your Amazon account.

Access and corrections

Members can edit their own onboarding details. Verification status, inventory assignments, and settlement records are controlled by NITERUN; members cannot create their own confirmed earnings. Contact chayson@niterun.com with a data access, correction, deletion, or privacy question. Requests are handled subject to applicable law, legitimate record-retention needs, and technical feasibility.

Consignment participation

Academy registration is free and does not establish an employment relationship, consignment contract, Amazon account approval, or a guarantee of inventory or earnings. Any actual consignment requires a separate approved written agreement, account verification, valid selling permissions, a funded fulfillment plan, and settlement terms. Amazon may impose seller account fees, FBA costs, and other charges. Independent sellers remain responsible for their own Amazon accounts and legal obligations; NITERUN remains responsible for its own conduct and contractual obligations.

AI and account connection

NIKO currently provides guided onboarding. Automated Amazon Seller Central access and financial data synchronization are not yet available. Any future Amazon connection will use an appropriately authorized integration; NITE Academy will not request the member's Amazon password. Automatic bank collection is also not currently activated and would require a separate, authorized payment process.

This is pilot-stage program information, not individualized legal, tax, or financial advice. Contact: chayson@niterun.com.
