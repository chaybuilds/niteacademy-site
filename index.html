05 / Clear expectations

Questions worth asking.

Do I need an LLC to apply?

No. Registration and licensing requirements depend on your activity and jurisdiction. We can explain general setup steps, but legal and tax advice must come from qualified professionals.

Will you find my customers?

Customer acquisition is not included or guaranteed. We can configure intake and CRM systems; you still need legitimate demand, marketing and delivery capability.

Do you own any part of my business?

Not under the standard implementation proposal. The buyer owns their accounts, data and client relationships; any different arrangement requires an explicit separate agreement.

Do I have to pay to join Discord?

No. Free lessons and community participation are separate from implementation services. Purchasing a service is not a requirement for paid-work opportunities.

What are the recurring costs?

Hosting, email, domains, payment processing, backups and other optional software depend on your setup and are disclosed before activation. Optional NITERUN operational support is separately agreed.

Can your AI run everything for me?

AI can assist with repetitive drafting, routing, forms and reporting. Real businesses still need owner decisions, human oversight, customers, quality delivery, security, and applicable professional licensing.
